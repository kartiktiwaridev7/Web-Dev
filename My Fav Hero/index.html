The Four Chapters

Dive into the epic journey of Krishna and Jadoo, divided into four legendary arcs.

01

The Discovery

Kid Krrish follows young Krishna, who discovers his father's alien-tech sundial watch and realizes he has superpowers. After defending himself against the evil Dr. Par, his journey begins.

02

The Alien Bond

Relocated to the mountains of Himachal by his grandmother, Krishna crosses paths with the alien Jadoo. Together, they form an unbreakable bond and begin to understand their true potential.

03

Rise of Shadows

Dr. Par's sinister forces close in, threatening the peace. Krishna must learn to harness his newfound abilities, combining his strength with Jadoo's extraterrestrial powers.

04

Earth's Protector

The final showdown. With the fate of the world hanging in the balance, Krishna dons the mask to become the ultimate symbol of hope and the line of defense against darkness.
